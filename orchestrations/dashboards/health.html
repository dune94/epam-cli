When a story fails a gate, the failure-analyst diagnoses it and routes a fix to one of three targets: a skill note appended to the responsible agent's profile, a brand-new tool script synthesized into .epam/dynamic-tools/, or occasionally a new agent profile. This page reads healing-events.jsonl, profiles.json vs. its restored floor, and the dynamic tools directory to show that learning loop directly.
