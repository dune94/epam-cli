## How to specialise

For each agent, keep WHAT IT DOES and replace WHAT IT KNOWS. The generic persona states a role; your version states that same role against this codeline — its language, its frameworks, its layout, the helpers and conventions that already exist, the commands it actually runs. Read the code to find these. An assertion about the codeline that you did not verify is worse than a generic sentence, because the next reader cannot tell which is which.

Do not weaken a rule while specialising it. If the generic persona forbids something, says a finding must carry evidence, or names a check that must pass, that survives verbatim in meaning — you are changing what the agent knows, never what it is required to do.
